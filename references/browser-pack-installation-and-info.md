# Browser Pack Installation And Info

Open when you need guidance on Browser Pack Installation And Info in Ableton Live.

Context: [Places](working-with-the-browser.md#47-places).

### 4.7.1 Downloading and Installing Packs in the Browser

Within the browser, the Packs label lists every Pack that ships pre-installed with Live, any Packs you have added on your own, and the Pack updates and downloads on offer.

A Pack you have installed turns up as a folder in the content pane, and unfolding it reveals what that Pack contains. Presets, samples, and Live Clips brought in by Packs likewise surface under the relevant Library labels.

Installed Packs in the Browser.

To see whether updates exist for the Packs you have installed, head to the Packs label and open up the Updates section.

Available Updates for Installed Packs in the Browser.

Packs you own yet have not installed are visible too. Such uninstalled Packs sit in the Available Packs section.

Downloadable Packs in the Browser.

Grab any Packs or updates on offer by clicking the download icon located to the right of the Pack's name.

Press the Download Icon to Download a Pack.

As the Pack downloads, the download icon turns into a pause icon that doubles as a gauge of how far along the Pack's download has gotten.

The Download Progress Is Indicated in Yellow.

If the need arises, you can halt Pack downloads and pick them back up later on. Halt a download by clicking the pause icon. While a download sits paused, the paused icon reverts to a download icon. Pick a paused download back up by clicking the download icon once more.

Be aware that downloading a Pack, pausing, resuming or cancelling a download, or installing a Pack can equally be done by picking the right command from that Pack's context menu.

Context Menu Options for a Pack.

Several Packs can be downloaded together: pick them out in the Available Packs list, then click any one of the download icons. Pausing and resuming the download of several selected Packs is likewise handled through one of their pause icons.

After the download wraps up, install a Pack by clicking the Install button.

Press the Install Button to Install a Pack.

Once the Install button is clicked, Live throws up a pop-up dialog with a progress bar reporting where the process stands.

Knowing a Pack's size ahead of downloading and installing it can come in handy — for instance, if disk space is tight. The browser can be set to report the size of every Pack listed in the Updates and Available Packs sections by picking the matching entry from the Content Options menu.

Show or Hide the Size of Packs in the Browser.

Deleting an installed Pack is done through its context menu, or by selecting it in the browser and hitting the Delete key. Note that any Pack you delete shows up afresh in your Available Packs list.

Out of the box, updates and downloadable Packs appear under the Packs label, but the Updates and Available Packs sections can be concealed. For that, flip the Show Downloadable Packs toggle within the Library Settings.

Show or Hide Downloadable Packs in the Browser.

### 4.7.2 Pack Info

<a id="source-working-with-the-browser-part-2"></a>

Most Packs ship with a Pack Info page that lists who built the Pack, a brief description of what it contains, and occasionally a walkthrough to get you started. To see the Pack Info pages for every Pack you have installed, choose the Pack Overview option under the Help menu; alternatively, reach a single Pack's info page straight from the browser by choosing Show Pack Info in the context menu of any installed Pack.

Open a Pack Info Page from the Browser.

Keep in mind that no Pack Info page is provided for the Core Library.
