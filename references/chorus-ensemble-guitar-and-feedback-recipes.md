### 28.8.1 Chorus-Ensemble Tips

A couple of pointers for working with Chorus-Ensemble:

- Run Ensemble mode with a rate of 1 Hz to 1.8 Hz and 100% Amount over dry guitars for that classic surf-rock tone.
- Automate the Feedback Invert toggle while Feedback sits above 90% to unleash huge swells of decaying oscillation.
