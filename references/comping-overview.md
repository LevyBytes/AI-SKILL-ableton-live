### 21. Comping

With comping, you can cherry-pick the strongest moments from each recorded take and merge them into one composite track (commonly called a “comp”). As you record, Live can spawn take lanes within a track, giving you a place to assemble your favorite sections. You might also keep alternate versions of a clip arrangement across several take lanes, or pull samples from your library onto take lanes and treat comping as a creative way to chop samples.
