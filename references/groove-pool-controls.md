### 14.1 Groove Pool

After a groove file has been applied, tweak how it behaves by editing its parameters within the _Groove Pool_. You open or close that pool from a drop-down entry in the browser view control, or with the Ctrl+Alt+6 (Win) / Cmd+Option+6 (Mac) shortcut.

The Groove Pool View Control.

Double-clicking a groove in the browser is another route: it sends the groove straight into the Groove Pool ahead of assigning it to any clip. Every groove brought in that way, plus those currently in use by clips, sits in the Groove Pool. Any “Inactive“ groove — one no clip is using — shows up with its parameters dimmed.
