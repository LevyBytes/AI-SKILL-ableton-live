# Live Audio Effect Reference (part 11)

> Ableton Live reference. Original prose; identifiers preserved verbatim.

1. Place Vocoder on whichever track holds your vocal material. That material may come from a clip carrying an already-recorded voice clip; alternatively, for treating a live vocal signal, route a microphone to a channel of your audio hardware and pick it as the track’s [input source](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/routing-and-i-o/#external-audio-inout).
2. On a separate track, add a synthesizer — [Analog](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#analog) is one example. As before, you might build a MIDI clip to feed that synthesizer, or simply perform it live.
3. Switch the vocoder’s Carrier chooser over to External.
4. Point the vocoder’s Audio From choosers at the synthesizer track. (The cleanest outcome comes from picking Post FX in the lower chooser.)
5. When the synthesizer and the vocal part are being produced on the fly, confirm that the Arm button sits enabled across both tracks.
6. Perform on the synthesizer while talking into the microphone. What you get back is the cadence of your words carrying the synthesizer’s tone color and frequency content. Should you want just the vocoded output, solo the voice track, which silences the ”normal” synthesizer track.

Note: outcomes tend to be strongest when the synthesizer tone is bright and packed with harmonics. Reaching for sawtooth-based patches helps the words come through more clearly. Should you crave still more brightness and definition, nudge the Unvoiced control, switch on Enhance, or do both.

#### 28.42.1.2 Formant Shifter

Configuring the Vocoder so the modulator doubles as its carrier turns it into an effective formant shifter. The steps:

1. Move the Carrier chooser to Modulator.
2. Bring the Depth up to 100%.
3. Turn on Enhance.

From there, audition assorted positions of the Formant knob to reshape the source’s character. To unlock further sound-design territory, work the assorted filterbank parameters too.
