### 40.4.1 Navigate Menu

The Navigate menu gives you a way into every one of Live’s main views plus the other navigation options. The bulk of the menu’s entries carry matching keyboard shortcuts as well. Open the Navigate menu with Alt+N (Win) / VO+M then N with VoiceOver (Mac).
