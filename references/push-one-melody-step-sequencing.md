### 34.6 Step Sequencing Melodies and Harmonies

On top of playing and recording in real time, your melodies and harmonies can be step sequenced as well. Press the Note Mode button a second time to switch over to the Melodic Sequencer. Doing so arranges the 8x8 pad grid like this:

The Pad Grid When Step Sequencing Pitches.

In the Melodic Sequencer, all eight pad rows let you drop notes into the clip. You can set the loop length and reach further step sequencing pages through the [loop length pads](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#adjusting-the-loop-length). The loop length pads can be reached briefly in the top row while the Note button is held.

The loop length pads can also be pinned in place. For that, hold Shift and tap the Note button. Be aware that Push 1 retains this locked/unlocked state per track. Press the Note button again to release the loop length pads.

With In Key chosen, every row maps to one of the pitches on offer in the scale you've currently selected. With Chromatic chosen, notes belonging to the key are lit while notes outside the key stay unlit. The light blue row — the bottom row to begin with — marks the root of the chosen key. Every column of pads stands for a step at the resolution fixed by the Scene/Grid buttons.

Just as in the real-time playing layout, a press of the Octave Up and Down button moves the span of notes on offer. The touch strip can serve to change the range too. Keep the Shift key held while you work the touch strip or press the Octave buttons to fine-tune the pitch range. Once you've adjusted the pitch range, or when you cross between the real-time and step sequencing layouts, the display flashes the available range for a moment.

The Display Shows the Range of Available Notes.

On top of that, brightly-lit touch strip lights point out the note range available at the moment, whereas dimly-lit touch strip lights signal that the clip holds notes inside the matching note range.

A further press of Note switches across to the [Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes layout](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#melodic-sequencer--32-notes).

Beyond adding and clearing notes, you can also tune the velocity and micro-timing of the notes, which the section on [step sequencing automation](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/using-push-1/#step-sequencing-automation) covers.
