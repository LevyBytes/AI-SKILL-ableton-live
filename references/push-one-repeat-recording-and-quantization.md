### 34.4.1 Recording with Repeat

Once Push 1’s Repeat button is turned on, holding a pad down plays or records an unbroken stream of evenly-spaced notes. That proves handy for laying down steady hi-hat patterns, as one example. Adjusting how hard your finger presses the pad shifts the loudness of the repeated notes.

Repeat Button.

You dial in the repeat rate with the Scene/Grid buttons. Keep in mind that Push 1 “remembers” the Repeat button’s on/off state and setting separately for each track.

Tap Repeat and let go quickly and the button stays lit. Press and keep it held, and it switches off the moment you release, which gives you momentary control over repeated notes.

Raise the Swing knob to bring swing into the repeated notes. As soon as your hand touches the knob, the display reports how much swing is set.

### 34.4.2 Quantizing

A press of Push 1’s Quantize button locks the notes in the chosen clip to the grid.

Quantize Button.

Hold Quantize down to adjust the quantization options:

Quantization Options.

Swing Amount governs how much swing gets applied to the notes being quantized. Keep in mind the Swing amount may be set either from Encoder 1 or from the dedicated Swing knob.

Quantize To picks the closest note value that notes get quantized toward, whereas Quantize Amount governs how far notes may be shifted away from where they started.

Switch on Record Quantize to have notes quantized automatically as you record, and tune the record quantization value with Encoder 8. Note that these controls line up with the settings of the Record Quantization chooser in Live’s Edit menu, so changes can be made either in Live or on Push 1.

For drum work, hold Quantize down and press a Drum Rack pad to quantize the notes of that drum alone within the current clip.
