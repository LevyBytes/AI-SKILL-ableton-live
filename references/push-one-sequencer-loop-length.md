### 34.6.1 Adjusting the Loop Length

The loop length controls let you fix the length of the clip’s loop and decide which slice of it shows up for viewing and editing in the melodic and drum step sequencers. Every loop length pad answers to a _page_ of steps, and how long a page runs hinges on the step resolution. With drums at the standard 16th note resolution, two pages of steps sit available at once, adding up to two bars. Under the Melodic Sequencer layout, a single page of eight steps is available at once, adding up to two beats. To alter the loop length, hold one pad and then tap a second pad or, to fix the loop length at exactly one page, give the matching pad a quick double-tap.

Each Loop Length Pad Corresponds to One Page.

Keep in mind that the page you _see_ need not be the page you _hear_. As you set the loop length, the pages refresh so the current play position (shown by the travelling green pad in the step sequencer section) stays in view at all times. In certain situations, though, you might prefer to switch off this auto-follow behavior. Say you want to edit one page of a longer loop while still letting the loop play across the length you set. For that, single-tap the pad answering to that page. Doing so “locks” the view onto that page and leaves the loop length untouched. To bring auto-follow back, just reselect the current loop. Note that single-tapping a page lying _outside_ the current loop sets the loop to that page on the spot.

Within the loop length section, the pad colors stand for the following:

- Unlit: this page falls outside the loop.
- White: this page lies inside the loop yet isn't on view at present in the step sequencer section.
- Dull yellow: this page is on view in the step sequencer section but isn't playing at present.
- Green: this is the page playing right now.

Should you need the loop length pads on hand often, you can pin them in place. For that, hold Shift and tap the Note button. Be aware that Push 1 retains this locked/unlocked state per track. Press the Note button again to release the loop length pads.

To copy what's on a sequencer page, hold Duplicate, press the loop length pad of the page you wish to copy, then press the loop length pad of the target page. Bear in mind this leaves any notes already on the target page in place and stacks the copied notes over them. To clear those notes first, hold Delete and tap the loop length pad of that page.
