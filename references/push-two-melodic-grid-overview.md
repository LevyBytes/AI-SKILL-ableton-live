### 35.7 Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes

Step sequencing and live playing are merged together in the Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes layout. Because it exposes several octaves alongside steps on one page, it shines when you're working out chords and harmonies to sequence. Longer phrases are another strong fit for it.
