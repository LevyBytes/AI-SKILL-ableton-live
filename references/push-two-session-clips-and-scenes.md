### 35.15 Controlling Live’s Session View

Hit Push 2’s Session button to cross over from Note Mode into Session Mode. Holding the Session button down toggles Session Mode for a moment, and letting it go drops you back to Note Mode. By the same token, holding Note while you are in Session Mode toggles Note Mode for a moment.

Session Button.

Under Session Mode, the 8x8 pad grid takes on the job of launching clips while the Scene/Grid Buttons launch scenes. A pad press fires the clip sitting at the matching spot in Live’s Session View. Where the track is already selected, pressing the button captures a fresh clip.

So that you can follow what is happening, the pads glow in assorted colors:

- Whatever color a clip carries, whether it is playing or stopped, shows on its pad.
- Clips that are playing throb in their own color.
- Clips being recorded throb between red and the clip’s color.

Clip colors are editable while you are in Session Mode. For that, hold Shift and press a pad holding a clip, then tap one of the outer-ring pads to assign that color to the selected clip.

Hitting the Mute or Solo buttons mutes or solos, in turn, whichever track is selected. With Mute or Solo held, a press on any track’s lower display button mutes or solos that particular track. Keeping Mute or Solo held down briefly latches it on; you can then let go and toggle tracks on or off merely by pressing the matching lower display buttons. A further press of Mute or Solo releases the lock.

Hitting the Stop Clip button halts the clip playing in whichever track is selected. Hold Stop Clip and press any track’s lower display button to halt the clip playing in that track.

Mute, Solo, and Stop Clip Buttons.

Keeping the Stop Clip button held down briefly latches it on. You can then let go of Stop Clip and halt clips just by pressing the lower display button of the track you wish to stop. With Stop Clip latched, the lower display buttons throb for whatever tracks hold a clip currently playing. A further press of Stop Clip releases the lock.

Halt every clip at once by holding Shift and then pressing Stop Clip.

Push 2 reports back what the software is doing, yet, crucially, the software likewise mirrors what is taking place on the hardware. Inside Live, the clip slots that Push 2’s pad grid is governing at the moment carry a colored border.

Copy a clip by holding the Duplicate button and pressing it. Keep Duplicate held and tap a different clip slot to drop the clip into place there.

Both the arrows and the Shift button widen the reach of the eight-by-eight grid.

- The up or down arrows shift you a scene at a time up or down. The Octave Up or Down buttons jump eight scenes up or down.
- The left and right arrow keys shift you a track at a time left or right. The Page left and right buttons jump eight tracks at a stride.

When you are in Session Mode with the focus on Clip Mode, the display sits “folded,” meaning every note within a clip stays on the display at all times. Seeing all of a clip’s notes lets you recognize it at a glance before you launch it. Select a clip without launching it by holding the Select button while tapping the pad that holds it.

As you record MIDI from an outside source — say a MIDI sequencer or MIDI keyboard — the folded display lets you watch every note coming in.

While in Session Mode, holding the Layout button briefly opens the Session Overview, covered in fuller detail just below.
