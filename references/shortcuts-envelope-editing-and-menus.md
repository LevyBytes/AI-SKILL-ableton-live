### 41.27 Editing Automation and Modulation Envelopes with the Keyboard

Breakpoints in automation and modulation envelopes can be added, modified, and removed straight from the computer keyboard. To read more on how this works, consult the [Editing Automation and Modulation Envelopes section](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/accessibility-and-keyboard-navigation/#editing-automation-and-modulation-envelopes) found in the Accessibility and Keyboard Navigation chapter.

### 41.28 Accessing Menus

Under Windows, any menu opens when you press Alt together with that menu’s initial letter (Alt+F opens “File,” for example). With a menu showing, the following are available:

- The up and down arrow keys to step among the entries in the menu.
- The right and left arrow keys to jump open the adjacent menu.
- Enter to confirm a menu entry.

On Mac, pressing Cmd+? reaches the individual menu entries. Doing so brings up a search field that offers menu entries as suggestions while you type. Move through those suggestions with the up and down arrow keys, then hit Enter to pick one.

### 41.29 Using Live’s Context Menu

Throughout various parts of Live, a context menu gives you fast access to frequently used actions and menu items. You bring it up by right-clicking (Win) / Ctrl-clicking (Mac) on whichever spot in the interface you want to act on. The menu can also be summoned with the Menu key or Shift+F10 under Windows, or VO+Shift+M on Mac. Keep in mind that this context menu will occasionally surface relevant options drawn from Live’s Settings. Adjust those with caution, since they reach beyond the item currently selected and touch the program’s overall settings as well.

A handful of commands surface nowhere but the context menu. That set includes a range of choices tied to [working with the browser](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/working-with-the-browser/#working-with-the-browser), the dedicated grid-marker entries used for [directing Auto-Warp](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/audio-clips-tempo-and-warping/#auto-warping-long-samples), fine-grained settings governing [zoom-adaptive and fixed grid line width](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/arrangement-view/#using-the-editing-grid), the copy-and-paste actions covering [Operator’s envelopes and oscillators](https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/live-instrument-reference/#operator), plus a great many commands specific to individual devices.
