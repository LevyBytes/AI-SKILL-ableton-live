### 1.1 The Ableton Team Says: Thank You

Live grew out of musicians who were after a better approach to composing, producing, and performing on a computer. Considerable work went into keeping Live approachable and enjoyable, while still letting you build tracks of boundless depth and refinement. Treat this manual as a companion for becoming comfortable with the full range of Live’s features and workflows.

Our wish is that Live proves a joy to work with and strengthens whatever you create.

Your Ableton Team.
