### 4.10 Adding Content from the Browser to a Live Set

Browser content can be brought into a Live Set through a number of approaches:

- You can drag items out of the browser and drop them onto tracks within either the Session or Arrangement View. When you instead drop browser content into the area to the right of Session View tracks, or beneath the Arrangement View tracks, Live builds a fresh track and deposits the new item(s) inside it.
- Within the Session View, a double-click — or a press of Enter — on a browser device drops it onto whichever track is active. The same gesture applied to a sample likewise sends it to the active track. For MIDI tracks a sample arrives wrapped in a Simpler device, whereas on audio tracks it lands in a clip slot.
- Over in the Arrangement View, double-clicking a browser device or sample (or hitting Enter on it) places that item onto whichever track happens to be selected at the time.
- It is equally possible to drag files straight into Live out of the Explorer (Win)/Finder (Mac).
